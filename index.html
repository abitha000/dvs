The Siddha Tradition

One of the oldest systems of medicine, born in Tamil Nadu.

Siddha is the medical wisdom of the Siddhars — sages who decoded the relationship between the body, the elements, and the cosmos. It is a living science: gentle, holistic, and deeply individualised.

01

Vatham · Pitham · Kapham

Siddha views health as the harmony of three humours. Imbalance is the seed of illness; restoring balance is the path back to wellness.

02

Herbs, Minerals & Lifestyle

Treatment draws on time-tested herbal formulations, mineral preparations, diet, and daily rhythm — never on suppression of symptoms.

03

Treat the person, not the disease

Every body is unique. Your prakriti, season, age, and inner climate all shape the medicine that will truly heal you.
